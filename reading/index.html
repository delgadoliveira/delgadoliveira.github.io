How this list is built

The selection logic is deliberately simple and kept in version control at scripts/fetch_papers.py, so the ranking can be inspected rather than taken on trust.

  • Foundations are hand-picked, metadata is not. I choose the arXiv identifiers; titles, authors, and abstracts are always fetched from the Hugging Face papers API. Nothing is transcribed by hand, so a citation cannot silently drift from the real paper.
  • Recent work is filtered, not mirrored. Candidates come from the daily feed plus targeted searches, then pass a keyword model with per-track weights, penalties for off-domain topics, and a minimum score. Most candidates are rejected.
  • Relevance ranks above popularity. Upvotes break ties; they never drive the ordering. A widely shared paper that does not match the track does not appear.
  • The automated half carries no endorsement. Foundations reflect a considered judgment, and each carries a note on what it establishes and what it leaves unsettled. The recent section is a filtered view of what is being published, deliberately left unannotated, and inclusion is not a claim that a paper is correct or important.
