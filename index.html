02 / Practice

Evaluation methodology.

My work treats evaluation as a sequence of complementary measurement problems, from controlled task-level analysis to randomized experiments and progressive deployment.

01

Agentic features, end to end

Task scoping, tool and skill design, grounding, retrieval, failure handling, guardrails, and instrumentation.

02

Offline evaluation design

Representative datasets, clear rubrics, human labeling, judge calibration, slice analysis, and tests for common failure modes.

03

Online experimentation

A/B tests designed around answerable questions, guardrail metrics, novelty effects, statistical uncertainty, and user-visible outcomes.

04

Progressive rollout evaluation

Sequencing evidence across deployment rings so small-population signals are interpreted carefully and issues surface early.

05

Query and retrieval quality

Improving what an AI system asks for and retrieves before optimizing the answer it generates.

06

Research to product decisions

Translating methods from papers and prototypes into repeatable decision procedures that product teams can run on schedule.
